# ARCHITECTURE'S MESSAGE

*"Every document holds a secret. Every sentence hides a possibility. And every detail may be the missing piece you've been searching for.*

Do not rush. Read every line carefully, examine every word, and question everything you encounter. The case files are not isolated stories; they are fragments of a much larger mystery.

Connect the dots. Uncover the hidden meanings. Piece together the evidence, for the answer to one case may hold the key to the next.

**Nothing is meaningless. Nothing is accidental.**

Proceed with caution, Detective. The truth reveals itself only to those who know how to look."
