6.The one who conquerd 
Steve walked into this world with nothing but his bare hands. Barren lands, endless jungle, ice and desert, he crossed them all to find a way out. But he was not alone. Beside him walked a crazy one. Everyone in this world knew his name, whether they wanted to or not.
Steve was the quiet engine of the pair. He was the one who never logged off. Days passed, the sun rose and fell on the same unbroken broadcast, and thousands of watchers lit up the sky like stars every time he blinked. They said he once gathered more witnesses in a single run than any other had ever held. They said his crew moved by one rule: any means possible.
They were desperate to leave and finish what they had started, but the world did not make it easy. They asked help from the gods of the berg and from the witnesses. Every monster had to fall: the dragon, the three-headed hunger, the guardian of the deep, the blind thing below.
And if either of them fell first, the world took everything back, and they woke up in the same place with nothing but their bare hands.
